# Notes

Training Metrics

## Height

Meaning: Vertical length of the plant measured from soil surface to the tallest point of the foliage.
Unit: cm

## Diameter

Maximum horizontal canopy spread of the plant
Unit: cm

## LeafArea

Meaning: Estimated total combined leaf surface area \
Unit cm^2

## FreshWeightShoot

Wet biomass of the above-ground shoot immediately after harvest
Unit: g

## Variety

Meaning: Cultivar or genetic variety of the lettuce 
Unit: category

## DryWeightShoot 

The weight of a plant's above-ground parts after all moisture has been removed 
Unit: g
